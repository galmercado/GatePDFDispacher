One click, no cookies: the “Send to Gate” bookmark

  1. Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar (once): Send to Gate
  2. Open the game's reservations page on {{ registration_host }} and sign in with Google as usual.
  3. Click the bookmark. A small window of this site opens - pick the event and press Import.

Your Google login stays on {{ registration_host }}; this site only receives the table's text. If you are not signed in here, sign in in the popup and click the bookmark again.
